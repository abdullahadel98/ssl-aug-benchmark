KEY INSIGHTS
================================================================================

1. TrivialAug Dominates:
   - Consistently best across all three SSL methods
   - BYOL TrivialAug (57.99%) is the single best model overall
   - 14.16pp improvement over BYOL Solo baseline

2. Method-Specific Differences:
   - BYOL: Strong with all augmentations, 14.16pp gap to baseline
   - SimCLR: Balanced performance, 4.79pp gap to baseline
   - DINO: Poor robustness, minimal augmentation benefit (4.19pp gap)

3. Style Transfer Performance:
   - Competitive with RandAug across methods
   - Best for SimCLR (54.95%)
   - Worse for DINO compared to baseline solo
   - Calibration trade-off: higher ECE than TrivialAug

4. Augmentation Benefit Ranking (by average improvement):
   1. TrivialAug: +5.35pp over baseline
   2. RandAug: +2.34pp over baseline
   3. Style Transfer: +3.53pp over baseline

5. DINO Anomaly:
   - All augmentations show minimal or negative effects
   - Solo baseline competitive with augmented variants
   - Suggests DINO may require different training regime or architecture tuning
